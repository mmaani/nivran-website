# PROJECT_SCOPE

## Project Definition
NIVRAN is a fragrance and body care brand project. This repository (`mmaani/nivran-website`) is exclusively for the Nivran storefront, brand operations, and supporting product/commerce workflows.

## What Belongs In This Repo
- Perfume and fragrance brand operations for Nivran.
- Fragrance/product development support artifacts.
- Packaging and labeling workflows for Nivran products.
- Product catalog, categories, pricing, and inventory logic.
- Brand assets and brand governance.
- E-commerce storefront logic (cart, checkout, order lifecycle, payment integrations).
- Marketing and launch workflows directly tied to Nivran.
- Operational systems directly related to Nivran customer and admin flows.

## What Does Not Belong In This Repo
- Zomorod Medical Supplies content, code, workflows, data, or terminology.
- QuickAIBuy content, code, workflows, data, or terminology.
- Medical supplies CRM or recruitment workflows.
- eBay integrations or marketplace automation workflows.
- Listing automation, marketplace profit engines, product matching AI, or marketplace inventory risk systems.

## Non-Mixing Rule
Never merge, reuse, or rebrand assets, code, data models, process language, or requirements from Zomorod Medical Supplies or QuickAIBuy in this repository.

If any instruction, file, context, or task indicates another project, stop immediately and report exactly:

`PROJECT MISMATCH DETECTED`
