# BRAND_GOVERNANCE

## Brand Ownership
All branding in this repo is for Nivran only.

## Governance Areas
- Brand naming and terminology must remain Nivran-specific.
- Tone and visual identity must remain consistent across storefront pages and assets.
- Packaging/labeling references must map to Nivran product lines only.
- Brand assets under `public/brand/` and related UI usage must not be repurposed for other projects.

## Prohibited Mixing
Do not introduce brand copy, terminology, or assets from:
- Zomorod Medical Supplies
- QuickAIBuy

If such mixing is requested or discovered, stop and report:

`PROJECT MISMATCH DETECTED`
